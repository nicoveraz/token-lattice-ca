\begin{abstract}
We turn a language model into a \emph{cellular automaton (CA) over token space} and use
it as a measurement instrument. A ring of $N$ token cells is updated in place by the
model's own windowed conditional $p_r(x_i \mid x_{i\pm r})$ at temperature $T$
(asynchronous random-order Glauber); the model \emph{is} the update rule, and the radius
$r$ of the conditioning window is a swept knob. Reading the model out as a dynamical
system rather than a next-token predictor exposes structure that static evaluation
misses. We report four black-box measurements, each certified against apparatus
confounds by a common-random-number (CRN) differential protocol. (1)~A \textbf{radius
law}: equilibrium \emph{local} statistics are nearly radius-blind, but corpus-consistent
\emph{longer-range} structure has an intermediate-radius optimum, visible only through a
repetition-robust metric. (2)~\textbf{Damage light cones} whose front velocity is set by
the conditioning radius, $v \propto r$, and is model-invariant. (3)~A \textbf{damping
length}: the asymptotic damage from a perturbation, once controlled for the model's
intrinsic diversity, shrinks as the window widens and grows with model capacity---the
larger models amplify perturbations beyond their own noise floor. A finite-size Lyapunov
exponent shows the \emph{early} dynamics are a purely kinematic axis shared by all
models, so the capacity signal lives entirely in the \emph{asymptotic} persistence:
velocity and stability are orthogonal, and only stability tracks capacity. (4)~\textbf{External
validity}: both the velocity law and the damping length replicate on an autoregressive
model driven through a one-sided causal window, so they are not artifacts of the masked
LM's globally-inconsistent joint. We calibrate against synthetic sources with known
transition matrices, where the attractor census recovers ground truth quantitatively and
discriminates sources. The contribution is the \textbf{instrument and the quantities it
measures}; the phenomena those quantities land on---criticality, computation at the edge
of chaos \citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024}---are decades
old, and we measure them from the outside rather than claim to discover them.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Language models are evaluated almost entirely as functions: given a context, how good is
the next-token distribution? But a masked or autoregressive conditional also \emph{defines
a dynamics}---iterate it in place over a lattice of tokens and one obtains a stochastic
cellular automaton whose rule is the model. Iterated-map views of LLMs have appeared
qualitatively \citep{logos_ca,telephone,paraphrase2cycle} and as mixing-time studies of
masked-LM Glauber dynamics \citep{glauber_mlm}. What has been missing is a
\emph{measurement instrument}: a small, controlled, calibrated apparatus that reads
structural properties out of the dynamics, with apparatus confounds explicitly certified
away. We build that instrument and sweep the one knob a token-space CA has that a
next-token evaluation does not---the \textbf{conditioning radius} $r$. A CA has a native
chaos vocabulary---damage spreading, light cones, Lyapunov exponents, attractor censuses
\citep{hanson1997computational}---and we import it wholesale. Our claims are modest about
\emph{what} is found and precise about \emph{how}: the edge-of-chaos and criticality ideas
the results touch are old \citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024};
we provide a black-box, token-space, generation-time \emph{measurement} of them in trained
LMs, plus a capacity-scaling result. The novel core is the instrument and its measurements.

\section{The instrument}
\textbf{Rule.} A window of $w=2r{+}1$ tokens with the center masked is fed to a masked LM;
the tempered center distribution is the kernel $p_r(x_i\mid x_{i\pm r})$. For an
autoregressive model (\S\ref{sec:ar}) the window is the $r$ cells to the left and the rule
is the next-token distribution---an order-$r$ Markov approximation, the causal analog of
the symmetric masked window. \textbf{Automaton.} $N$ ring cells, async random-order
single-site Glauber (synchronous updates manufacture period-2 blinkers and are only an
apparatus arm \citep{paraphrase2cycle}); special tokens forbidden as emissions.
\textbf{CRN coupling.} Twin lattices sharing initial state, update order, and the uniform
stream driving inverse-CDF sampling differ only in the factor under test; the \emph{null}
arm must diverge by exactly zero (it does, on both masked and autoregressive paths),
certifying the coupling. \textbf{Four pillars:} $T\times r$ phase sweeps; damage spreading
(block flips, CRN twins, ignition probability separate from spread); attractor census
(random soup to quasi-stationarity, validated against a corpus); and differential
certification---attribute at the level of \emph{statistics}, never trajectories (under
chaos, trajectory divergence saturates for signal and apparatus alike, so only a statistic
that nulls under apparatus swaps and moves under model swaps is a model reading).

\textbf{Two confounds shape every metric.} \emph{Repetition}: a lattice looping one corpus
$n$-gram scores high overlap for free, so structure uses a repetition-robust variant
(distinct corpus $n$-grams). \emph{Diversity} (its stability analog): a degenerate
low-entropy lattice snaps a perturbation back trivially---low damage for the wrong
reason---so damage is normalized by the model's own diversity floor (\S\ref{sec:repair}).
We validate on a tiny transformer trained from scratch on tinyshakespeare (census checked
against the actual training corpus), then scale to \texttt{bert-tiny/mini/base} and
\texttt{Pythia-160m/410m} \citep{pythia}. A free control: the temperature ``phase
transition'' is, on a finite-size scan, a \emph{crossover}, not a true transition (the
order-parameter susceptibility self-averages, $\chi_{\mathrm{peak}}\propto 1/N$),
disciplining all ``transition'' language.

\section{Results}
\subsection{A radius law}
Sweeping $r$ at fixed $T$, the \emph{local} statistic (corpus-bigram fraction) is nearly
radius-blind. \emph{Longer-range} corpus consistency is not: raw 3- and 4-gram overlap
rises with $r$, but under the repetition-robust metric the signal \emph{peaks at an
intermediate radius} ($r\approx4$) and falls by $r{=}16$---the apparent large-$r$ growth
was collapse into repetition. Certification: holding the special-token windowing scheme
fixed, the cross-model radius-profile difference far exceeds the distribution-preserving
apparatus floor (so the profile is a model effect), but the scheme itself moves the profile
as much as a model change, so cross-model claims must hold the scheme fixed
(Fig.~\ref{fig:radius}).

\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{phaseA_radius.png}
\caption{Radius certification. Cross-model radius profiles at a fixed scheme exceed the
distribution-preserving apparatus floor (model effect), but the special-token scheme is a
first-class apparatus of comparable magnitude.}\label{fig:radius}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Damage light cones: $v\propto r$}
A 3-site block flip under CRN produces a cone whose front advances ballistically at
$v\approx 1.6,3.5,7.7,\dots$ sites/sweep for $r=1,2,4,\dots$. A plateau at large $r$ on
small rings is finite-size (the front fills the ring in $\sim$2 sweeps) and lifts on an
$N\in\{48,96,192,384\}$ scan ($v=11.5\!\to\!23.5\!\to\!47.5$ at $r{=}16$). The unclipped
law $v\propto r$ is \textbf{model-invariant}: interaction range is a kinematic property of
the apparatus, not the model (Fig.~\ref{fig:velocity}). We do \emph{not} claim this law as
new: a ballistic damage front whose velocity is set by the neighborhood range is the
classic CA light cone---the maximal Lyapunov exponent equals the damage-front velocity
\citep{bagnoli1992damage}, finite-range interactions give a constant range-set velocity by
the Lieb--Robinson bound \citep{lieb1972finite}, and butterfly velocities of decorrelator
fronts are standard in chaotic CA \citep{butterfly_ca}. Our point is that the LM
token-lattice \emph{behaves as a bona-fide CA}---it exhibits this light cone---and that the
velocity is model-invariant, i.e.\ a shared kinematic axis (contrast \S\ref{sec:repair}).

\begin{figure}[t]\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{phaseA_velocity.png}
\caption{Damage-front velocity vs.\ ring size. The small-ring plateau is finite-size
wraparound; $v\propto r$ continues once unclipped.}\label{fig:velocity}
\end{figure}

\subsection{The damping length, kinematics $\perp$ stability}\label{sec:repair}
Perturb, evolve under CRN, and ask how much damage \emph{persists}. The raw answer is
diversity-confounded, so we normalize by a \textbf{diversity floor} \dz{}: the asymptotic
drift of twins sharing the settled initial state but evolving under \emph{independent}
noise with \emph{no} flip. Because \dz{} is a full radius-$r$ run it propagates at the same
$v\propto r$, so $\dn=D/\dz$ cancels \emph{both} the diversity and the kinematic terms.
Then (Fig.~\ref{fig:repair}): \dn{} rises with radius (damping length shrinks as the window
widens; the raw ``recovery'' at large $r$ was collapse); capacity orders it monotonically
(mean \dn{} $0.672<0.880<0.904$ for tiny/mini/base), the larger models crossing $\dn>1$
(amplification beyond the noise floor). The ordering fights the normalization (a diverse
model's higher floor should deflate its \dn) and is velocity-immune (matched $r$), so it is
neither diversity nor kinematics. A finite-size Lyapunov exponent $\lambda$ (early
log-separation slope) is \textbf{model-invariant} ($\lambda_{\max}\approx0.74$--$0.77$ for
all three) and universally positive: early dynamics are a kinematic axis set by the radius,
and the capacity signal lives entirely in the asymptotic \dn. That $\lambda$ tracks the
damage-front velocity is itself the classic CA relation \citep{bagnoli1992damage}; our
contribution is the \emph{decomposition}---that this kinematic Lyapunov axis is universal
(model-invariant) while a distinct \emph{asymptotic} axis, \dn, is what tracks capacity. \emph{Reading (as
measurement)}: $\dn>1$ is a chaos signature; the smallest model reads ``stable'' because it
is \emph{frozen} (collapses to a dead repetitive attractor), the larger ones ``sensitive''
because \emph{expressive}. The capacity axis traces a stability$\leftrightarrow$expressiveness
tradeoff---the long-hypothesized edge-of-chaos picture
\citep{bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024} measured black-box, with a capacity axis added.

\begin{figure}[t]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{repair_scale.png}
\caption{The damping length. Left: raw damage $D$ vs.\ $r$. Middle: diversity+velocity
controlled $\dn=D/\dz$---rises with $r$, ordered by capacity, $\dn>1$ for the larger models.
Right: \dn{} vs.\ diversity (flat $\Rightarrow$ controlled).}\label{fig:repair}
\end{figure}

\subsection{External validity: the autoregressive port}\label{sec:ar}
Both load-bearing measurements replicate on \texttt{Pythia-160m} driven through a one-sided
\emph{causal} window (null CRN divergence exactly zero). Velocity$\propto r$ holds
($v=5.8,7.7,11.5$ for $r=2,4,8$; $r{=}1$ does not propagate), and the damping length holds
(\dn{} climbs $0.001\!\to\!0.98$ across $r$). The capacity$\to$sensitivity climb replicates
too: at the sub-saturation radius $r{=}2$, mean \dn{} rises $0.64\to0.73$ from Pythia-160m
to 410m. Because the masked-LM joint is globally inconsistent \citep{glauber_mlm} while the
autoregressive joint is consistent, replication across constructions is the strongest
evidence the phenomena are properties of trained-LM token dynamics, not artifacts of the
masked construction. We compare at the level of the \emph{trend}: causal-context healing is
a different object from bidirectional healing.

\subsection{Calibration against ground truth}
On three synthetic first-order Markov sources sharing a vocabulary but with different sparse
transition matrices $P_a/P_b/P_c$, a model trained on each and censused yields an empirical
bigram transition $Q_X$ matching its own $P_X$ (row total-variation $0.22$ vs.\ a
random-lattice baseline $0.91$) and \emph{not} the others (cross-TV $0.95$): the census
recovers each model's own priors quantitatively and discriminates sources
(Fig.~\ref{fig:calib})---the honest trained-model analog of a sampler oracle
\citep{sampler_oracle}. On natural corpora the recovery is qualitative but structurally
identical: the deepest attractors are the corpus's \emph{format skeleton}. Vocabulary
compression corrupts this---a word-level \texttt{<unk>} becomes attractor material and
inflates recovery through artifact-to-artifact matching; byte-level BPE removes it.

\includegraphics[width=0.5\linewidth]{calib_discrimination.png}
\caption{Calibration. Row total-variation between each model's censused bigram transition
$Q_X$ and every true $P_Y$: diagonal (self-recovery) $\approx0.22$, off-diagonal
$\approx0.95$---quantitative recovery and clean discrimination.}\label{fig:calib}
\end{figure}

\section{Related work}
A novelty check (102-agent adversarial harness) places our contribution precisely: the
\emph{instrument} is largely novel at the method level, but its \emph{substrate} is not,
and novelty is uneven across claims. \textbf{Shared substrate (the exposed flank).}
\citet{glauber_mlm} already recasts a masked LM as iterated masked-token resampling---a
Glauber Markov chain on token sequences---the exact substrate we iterate. It measures
mixing time and metastability, \emph{not} damage spreading, light-cone velocity, a
damping length, or a finite-size Lyapunov exponent, and it couples chains with
\emph{maximal} coupling, provably distinct from our common-random-number coupling. Our
novelty therefore rests on the \emph{measurement layer}, not on the LM-as-iterated-token-CA
framing, which we do not claim. \textbf{Edge-of-chaos (claim of \S\ref{sec:repair} is
partially anticipated).} Measuring proximity to the edge of chaos and tying it to
capability is canonical \citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real} and has been
shown for trained transformers via Lyapunov exponents of self-attention Jacobians
\citep{lyap_selfattn}, and named for LLMs generally \citep{edgeofchaos2024,qle}. We claim
neither the concept nor the criticality$\leftrightarrow$capability link; our novelty is the
\emph{token-space} finite-size Lyapunov / damping-length measurement and the explicit
model-size$\to$sensitivity axis, which \citet{lyap_selfattn} (continuous hidden space,
performance-correlated) does not report. \textbf{The CA light cone is classic (\S3.2 is import, not discovery).} A ballistic
damage front whose velocity is set by the neighborhood range, and the identity
Lyapunov$=$front-velocity, are textbook CA / statistical-physics
\citep{bagnoli1992damage,lieb1972finite,butterfly_ca}; we import them and show the LM
token-lattice exhibits them (behaves as a bona-fide CA) and that the velocity is
model-invariant---not that the law is new. \textbf{Perturbation propagation.}
\citet{sparc} formalizes an error-propagation operator on AR residual streams with a
$\rho(F_T)\!\ge\!1$ criticality threshold (the top-Lyapunov boundary) in activation space,
adjacent but not a token-space CA; \citet{quiver} operates on compound-AI pipeline graphs,
sharing vocabulary but no method. \textbf{Terminology.} We avoid ``repair''---taken by
self-repair / the Hydra effect \citep{hydra,selfrepair} for internal component
compensation---and ``self-correction'' \citep{sparc}, both categorically different from our
spatial damping length. \textbf{Other.} \citet{sampler_oracle} is the calibration/oracle
kin (our synthetic-Markov recovery is its trained-model analog); temperature-criticality
work \citep{ar_tempcrit,phase_detect} makes $T$ a calibration anchor, not a contribution;
the census descends from \citet{hanson1997computational}; early low-order crystallization
is consistent with distributional simplicity bias \citep{simplicitybias}, read black-box.
\emph{(Pre-submission: full-paper reads of \citet{glauber_mlm} and \citet{quiver}; verify
all 2026-preprint titles/IDs; see \texttt{results/deep\_research\_novelty.md}.)}

\section{Limitations}
Masked-LM local conditionals are globally inconsistent, so the CA is a well-defined
stochastic dynamical system but not a sampler of any joint; all claims are properties of
the dynamics. The real-model census is proxy-validated (WikiText, out-of-training), a lower
bound; ground-truth calibration is on synthetic sources only. Rings are small ($N\le384$),
the ladder short (three masked, two autoregressive), and the AR capacity trend rests on two
models with only the sub-saturation radius discriminating (the 410m largest window OOM'd on
16\,GB). The special-token windowing scheme is a first-class apparatus factor to hold fixed;
temperature scales are not comparable across masked and autoregressive constructions.

\section{Conclusion}
Reading a language model as a cellular automaton over its own token space, with the
conditioning radius as a swept knob and CRN perturbations as the probe, yields calibrated,
apparatus-certified measurements static evaluation does not: a radius law, a model-invariant
damage velocity, and a diversity- and velocity-controlled damping length that shortens with
capacity as the dynamics move toward the chaotic side---replicated on an autoregressive
model and quantified against ground truth. The instrument is the contribution; the phenomena
it measures are old and deep, and that is exactly why measuring them cleanly, from the
outside, is worth doing.
