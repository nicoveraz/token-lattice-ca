\begin{abstract}
We turn a language model into a \emph{cellular automaton (CA) over token space}---a ring of
token cells updated in place by the model's own windowed conditional $p_r(x_i\mid x_{i\pm r})$,
with common-random-number (CRN) damage spreading as the probe---and develop it into a
\emph{validated} black-box measurement instrument. The central move is \textbf{validation by
reproduction}: before measuring a language model, the instrument recovers established
dynamical metrics on systems where they are known. Its CRN damage-spreading estimator
recovers the ordered/edge/chaotic criticality classes of elementary CA rules, the
damage-spreading boundary of a stochastic discrete PCA with a published phase diagram, and---
through its attractor census---the known transition matrices of synthetic Markov sources
(self-recovery total-variation $0.22$ vs.\ cross $0.95$). We are explicit about what the
smooth-limit rungs do and do not establish: a renormalized tangent estimator reproduces the
logistic map's analytic Lyapunov exponent to $O(\epsilon)$, but that agreement is a property
of the infinitesimal limit, and a token flip is $O(1)$ in a discrete alphabet---so we keep
that rung as a \emph{unit test of the growth-rate arithmetic}, not as validation of the
instrument. Every measurement is certified against apparatus
confounds by an exact-zero-null CRN protocol and attributed at the level of \emph{statistics},
not trajectories. Applied to trained LMs, the validated instrument yields black-box
criticality measurements not otherwise available from weights-free access: a \textbf{token-space
Lyapunov exponent} and a diversity-controlled \textbf{damping length} of generation, an
\textbf{effective interaction radius} at which the model builds structure ($r{\approx}4$), a
model-invariant \textbf{damage light cone}, and an \textbf{attractor census} of internalized
structure. Finally we map a \emph{boundary}: a controlled cross-level study (two model
families, two within-model axes) shows the black-box token-space criticality does \emph{not}
proxy white-box activation-space criticality---they are distinct, non-proxying levels,
because the activation measure is architectural. The contribution is a \textbf{validated
instrument and the quantities it measures}; the phenomena it lands on---criticality,
computation at the edge of chaos
\citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024}---are decades old, and we
measure them from the outside rather than claim to discover them.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Language models are evaluated almost entirely as functions: given a context, how good is
the next-token distribution? But a masked or autoregressive conditional also \emph{defines
a dynamics}---iterate it in place over a lattice of tokens and one obtains a stochastic
cellular automaton whose rule is the model. Iterated-map views of LLMs have appeared
qualitatively \citep{logos_ca,telephone,paraphrase2cycle} and as mixing-time studies of
masked-LM Glauber dynamics \citep{glauber_mlm}; the phenomena a CA vocabulary names---damage
spreading, light cones, Lyapunov exponents, attractor censuses
\citep{hanson1997computational}---are decades old. What has been missing is not a new
phenomenon but a \emph{measurement instrument whose readings can be trusted}: a small,
controlled apparatus, calibrated against ground truth, with apparatus confounds certified
away.

This paper's organizing principle is \textbf{validation by reproduction}. We build the
instrument---a token-lattice CA with CRN damage spreading (\S\ref{sec:instrument})---and,
before turning it on a language model, show that it recovers \emph{known} dynamical metrics
on systems where the answer is established (\S\ref{sec:validation}): the criticality classes
of elementary CA rules, a stochastic PCA's published damage-spreading boundary, the
smooth-limit growth-rate arithmetic on the logistic map and coupled-map
lattice, the criticality classes of elementary CA rules, and the known transition matrices of
synthetic Markov sources. An instrument that reproduces exact values on known systems can be
\emph{trusted} on an unknown one. Only then do we report the new, weights-free measurements it
yields on trained LMs (\S\ref{sec:lm}): a token-space Lyapunov exponent, a damping length, an
effective interaction radius, a damage light cone, and an attractor census. Finally we map the
instrument's \emph{boundary}---the cross-level regime where the black-box token-space reading
provably decouples from white-box internals (\S\ref{sec:crosslevel}). The edge-of-chaos and
criticality ideas the results touch are old
\citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024}; our contribution is the
validated black-box \emph{measurement} of them, not their discovery.

\paragraph{Simplicity and tested reliability.} A deliberate choice runs through the paper: we
use only \emph{simple, mature} primitives from cellular-automaton and dynamical-systems
theory---single-site damage spreading, the damage-spreading Lyapunov exponent
\citep{bagnoli1992damage}, the light cone \citep{lieb1972finite}, the attractor census
\citep{hanson1997computational}---rather than bespoke machinery. Each has been used and tested
for decades, and the value of transferring them is precisely that they are \emph{understood}:
their expected behaviour on known systems is a fixed benchmark. That is what the validation
ladder exploits (\S\ref{sec:validation})---reliability here is not asserted, it is
\emph{demonstrated} by reproducing exact known values. The stance of the paper is a
disciplined transfer of a reliable field's instruments to language models, with every reading
either calibrated against ground truth or certified against apparatus confounds, and with the
one place the transfer fails reported as plainly as the places it succeeds.

\section{The instrument}\label{sec:instrument}
\textbf{Rule.} A window of $w=2r{+}1$ tokens with the center masked is fed to a masked LM;
the tempered center distribution is the kernel $p_r(x_i\mid x_{i\pm r})$. For an
autoregressive model (\S\ref{sec:ar}) the window is the $r$ cells to the left and the rule
is the next-token distribution---an order-$r$ Markov approximation, the causal analog of
the symmetric masked window. \textbf{Automaton.} $N$ ring cells, async random-order
single-site Glauber (synchronous updates manufacture period-2 blinkers and are only an
apparatus arm \citep{paraphrase2cycle}); special tokens forbidden as emissions.
\textbf{CRN coupling.} Twin lattices sharing initial state, update order, and the uniform
stream driving inverse-CDF sampling differ only in the factor under test; the \emph{null}
arm must diverge by exactly zero (it does, on both masked and autoregressive paths),
certifying the coupling. \textbf{Four pillars:} $T\times r$ phase sweeps; damage spreading
(block flips, CRN twins, ignition probability separate from spread); attractor census
(random soup to quasi-stationarity, validated against a corpus); and differential
certification---attribute at the level of \emph{statistics}, never trajectories (under
chaos, trajectory divergence saturates for signal and apparatus alike, so only a statistic
that nulls under apparatus swaps and moves under model swaps is a model reading).

\textbf{Two confounds shape every metric.} \emph{Repetition}: a lattice looping one corpus
$n$-gram scores high overlap for free, so structure uses a repetition-robust variant
(distinct corpus $n$-grams). \emph{Diversity} (its stability analog): a degenerate
low-entropy lattice snaps a perturbation back trivially---low damage for the wrong
reason---so damage is normalized by the model's own diversity floor (\S\ref{sec:repair}).
We validate on a tiny transformer trained from scratch on tinyshakespeare (census checked
against the actual training corpus), then scale across a masked family (\texttt{bert-tiny}
through \texttt{bert-large}, $L{=}2$--$24$) and two autoregressive families
(\texttt{Pythia-14M}--\texttt{1B} \citep{pythia} and \texttt{GPT-2} small--xl). A free control: the temperature ``phase
transition'' is, on a finite-size scan, a \emph{crossover}, not a true transition (the
order-parameter susceptibility self-averages, $\chi_{\mathrm{peak}}\propto 1/N$),
disciplining all ``transition'' language.

\section{Validation: reproducing known metrics}\label{sec:validation}
An instrument is only as good as its calibration. Before applying the CA/damage-spreading
apparatus to a language model---whose ``true'' dynamical metrics are unknown---we check it
against systems where the answer is known, and we are explicit about which properties each
rung does and does not exercise (Fig.~\ref{fig:ladder}). The ladder is ordered by how much of
the instrument's actual regime it shares: smooth-limit arithmetic, then \emph{discrete} state
with a finite perturbation---the regime the token instrument occupies.

\textbf{(1) The logistic map: a unit test of the growth-rate arithmetic, not a validation of
the instrument.} A renormalized tangent estimator---two orbits differing by $\epsilon$, with
the separation rescaled to $\epsilon$ after every step---recovers the analytic exponent
$\lambda(r)=\langle\ln|r(1-2x)|\rangle$ of $x\mapsto rx(1-x)$ across the bifurcation diagram
to machine precision at $\epsilon{=}10^{-9}$. We state plainly why that agreement is not
evidence about damage spreading: because the twin is re-anchored to the reference orbit each
step, $\log(d/\epsilon)=\log|f'(x)|+O(\epsilon)$, so the estimator \emph{is} a
finite-difference evaluation of the derivative it is compared against. An $\epsilon$-sweep
confirms the error is $O(\epsilon)$ (log--log slope $0.79$ over six decades; Fig.~\ref{fig:eps}).
\textbf{A token flip is $O(1)$ in a discrete alphabet---there is no $\epsilon\!\to\!0$ limit in
token space}---so this rung certifies only the smooth limit. Removing the renormalization and
using a finite perturbation, which is the instrument's actual regime, leaves an irreducible
bias of $\approx\!0.15$ nats/step that does \emph{not} vanish as $\epsilon\to0$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:eps}, flat curve): the windowed fit on a saturating separation, not the
perturbation size, sets the error. The weight-bearing rungs are therefore (3) and (4).

\textbf{(2) A coupled-map lattice (spatial, still smooth).} A ring of diffusively coupled
logistic maps recovers the single-site $\ln 2$ at zero coupling and a monotone reduction with
coupling. This estimator also renormalizes, so it inherits the caveat above; it extends the
arithmetic to a spatially-extended system but not to discrete state.

\textbf{(3) Elementary CA criticality classes.} Driving the identical damage-spreading
estimator (async updates, CRN twins, \texttt{lyap\_from\_cone}) with classical elementary CA
rules of \emph{known} class recovers the criticality ordering: group-mean $\lambda_{\mathrm
{ca}}$ is $-0.32$ (ordered: Rules 128/232/4) $<$ $+0.19$ (edge/complex: 110/54) $<$ $+0.26$
(chaotic: 30/150/22). The discriminating quantity is the \emph{ignition probability}---the
fraction of lattices in which a single-site flip survives at all (ordered $0.05$ vs.\ edge
$0.67$, chaotic $0.68$)---rather than $\lambda$ itself: damage in a discrete CA is bimodal,
so a $\lambda$ averaged over ignited and extinguished runs measures the mixture. Conditional
on ignition, edge and chaotic do not separate ($p{=}0.07$), so we claim only the coarse
ordered-vs-rest split.

\textbf{(4) Synthetic Markov sources (known transition matrices).} The instrument's attractor
census, run on tiny models trained on three synthetic first-order Markov sources with known
sparse matrices $P_a,P_b,P_c$, recovers each model's own matrix (row total-variation $0.22$)
and not the others (cross $0.95$; random baseline $0.91$)---quantitative recovery of a
ground-truth generative rule and clean source discrimination (details in \S\ref{sec:census}).

The rungs that share the instrument's regime---discrete state, finite $O(1)$ perturbation, no
renormalization---are (3) and (4), and those are what license its use on language models. The
smooth-limit rungs are retained as arithmetic unit tests and labelled as such.

\section{New measurements on language models}\label{sec:lm}
Pointed at a trained LM, the validated instrument yields the quantities below. We are precise
about what is new: several of these \emph{quantities} have been identified in concurrent work
(a critical sampling temperature
\citep{critical_temp,critical_temp2,critical_temp3,ar_tempcrit,phase_detect}; edge-of-chaos
and Lyapunov framings \citep{bertschinger2004real,lyap_selfattn}). What we contribute is not
the concepts but a single, CRN-certified, ground-truth-calibrated \emph{route} to them from
weights-free access---the same estimator validated in \S\ref{sec:validation}---together with a
few apparatus-level observations (the radius law, the confound taxonomy) that are specific to
the token-lattice construction.

\subsection{A radius law}
Sweeping $r$ at fixed $T$, the \emph{local} statistic (corpus-bigram fraction) is nearly
radius-blind. \emph{Longer-range} corpus consistency is not: raw 3- and 4-gram overlap
rises with $r$, but under the repetition-robust metric the signal \emph{peaks at an
intermediate radius} ($r\approx4$) and falls by $r{=}16$---the apparent large-$r$ growth
was collapse into repetition. Certification: holding the special-token windowing scheme
fixed, the cross-model radius-profile difference far exceeds the distribution-preserving
apparatus floor (so the profile is a model effect), but the scheme itself moves the profile
as much as a model change, so cross-model claims must hold the scheme fixed
(Fig.~\ref{fig:radius}).

\subsection{Damage light cones: $v\propto r$}
A 3-site block flip under CRN produces a cone whose front advances ballistically at
$v\approx 1.6,3.5,7.7,\dots$ sites/sweep for $r=1,2,4,\dots$. A plateau at large $r$ on
small rings is finite-size: the front fills the ring in $\sim$2 sweeps, so the velocity
measured there is merely the wraparound ceiling $\approx N/4$ (the values $11.5,23.5,47.5$
for $N{=}48,96,192$ at $r{=}16$ are exactly $N/4$, not an unclipped law). Only the largest
ring escapes clipping at $r{=}16$ ($N{=}384$), and there the velocity ($41.1$) sits
\emph{below} the smaller-ring ceilings---confirming they \emph{were} ceilings---while the
unclipped points are mildly \emph{super}-linear ($\approx1.5r$ at $r{=}4$ rising to
$\approx2.6r$ at $r{=}16$). So we do not claim a clean $v\propto r$; what is robust and
\textbf{model-invariant} is that the front velocity grows monotonically with the
conditioning radius---interaction range is a kinematic property of the apparatus, not the
model (Fig.~\ref{fig:velocity}). We do \emph{not} claim even the qualitative law as new: a ballistic damage front whose velocity is set by the neighborhood range is the
classic CA light cone---the maximal Lyapunov exponent equals the damage-front velocity
\citep{bagnoli1992damage}, finite-range interactions give a constant range-set velocity by
the Lieb--Robinson bound \citep{lieb1972finite}, and butterfly velocities of decorrelator
fronts are standard in chaotic CA \citep{butterfly_ca}. Our point is that the LM
token-lattice \emph{behaves as a bona-fide CA}---it exhibits this light cone---and that the
velocity is model-invariant, i.e.\ a shared kinematic axis (contrast \S\ref{sec:repair}).

wraparound; $v\propto r$ continues once unclipped.}\label{fig:velocity}

\subsection{The damping length}\label{sec:repair}
Perturb, evolve under CRN, and ask how much damage \emph{persists}. The raw answer is
diversity-confounded: a low-entropy lattice snaps back for free. We therefore report the
perturbation damage $D$ alongside a \textbf{diversity floor} \dz{}---the asymptotic drift
of twins sharing the settled initial state but evolving under \emph{independent} noise with
\emph{no} flip---and their ratio $\dn=D/\dz$. Two caveats on the normalization, stated
plainly because they bound the reading. \emph{First}, $D$ and \dz{} use different couplings
($D$ common-random-number, \dz{} independent noise), so \dn{} is a controlled diagnostic,
not a ratio of like quantities; the natural CRN floor is the null itself ($=0$). \emph{Second},
at large $r$ the rise in \dn{} is \textbf{denominator-driven}: raw $D$ already \emph{falls}
(tiny, $T{=}0.7$: $0.54\!\to\!0.39$ from $r{=}4$ to $r{=}16$) while \dz{} falls faster, so
``the damping length keeps shrinking'' at large $r$ partly reflects the floor collapsing,
not a stronger perturbation response.

Read as a floor-relative diagnostic (Fig.~\ref{fig:repair}), \dn{} rises with radius: the
token-space damping length shortens as the window widens. This is the measurement we
report---a diversity-controlled, weights-free error-persistence length of generation. A
capacity-linked gap does appear among masked models (mean \dn{} tiny $0.672<$ mini
$0.880\approx$ base $0.904$), but we \emph{do not build on it}: it rests on two seeds and one
toy family, the only clean separation is tiny $\ll$ \{mini,\,base\}, and an earlier
``$p<10^{-4}$'' statistic for it was pseudoreplicated (retracted---see Limitations and the
repository audit). We likewise withdraw a previously-reported kinematics-versus-stability
decomposition, which held only at the single grid cell where the early Lyapunov exponent
saturates. Whether capacity sets a stability$\leftrightarrow$expressiveness axis
\citep{bertschinger2004real,edgeofchaos2024} is left open, pending a seed-level rebuild; the
damping length itself stands as a measurement independent of that question.

controlled $\dn=D/\dz$---rises with $r$; $\dn\!\gtrsim\!1$ for the larger models.

\subsection{External validity: the autoregressive port}\label{sec:ar}
Both load-bearing measurements replicate on \texttt{Pythia-160m} driven through a one-sided
\emph{causal} window (null CRN divergence exactly zero). Velocity$\propto r$ holds
($v=5.8,7.7,11.5$ for $r=2,4,8$; $r{=}1$ does not propagate), and the damping length holds
(\dn{} climbs $0.001\!\to\!0.98$ across $r$). The capacity$\to$sensitivity result, however,
does \textbf{not} robustly carry over. Across four Pythia sizes with 5 seeds (mean \dn{} at
$r{=}2$, $\pm$SE): $0.41{\pm}.08$ (70m) $\approx 0.41{\pm}.08$ (160m) $< 0.56{\pm}.06$ (410m)
$> 0.47{\pm}.08$ (1b)---non-monotone, peaking at 410m and dropping at 1b, with an
insignificant size-rank correlation (Spearman $\rho{=}0.17$, $p{=}0.29$; only 160m$\to$410m
is marginal, $p{=}0.06$). So the masked-side capacity ordering (\S\ref{sec:repair}) is
masked-specific in this data, not construction-independent; neither construction extends it
monotonically (masked saturates, AR reverses). What replicates on AR is the \emph{instrument}
(velocity grows with $r$; the damping length rises with $r$), not the capacity scaling. The
masked-LM local conditionals are globally inconsistent \citep{glauber_mlm}; the
autoregressive rule here is a \emph{consistent} order-$r$ causal kernel---but resampled in
place on a periodic ring, so it too is not a sampler of the model's true left-to-right
joint. Both constructions are thus windowed, in-place-resampled ring dynamics rather than
the model's actual distribution. Replication across them is evidence that the radius-level
measurements are not tied to the masked windowing---not proof about either joint. We compare
at the level of the \emph{trend}, since causal-context healing is a different object from
bidirectional healing. (The AR means pool two temperature regimes with very different
\dn{} scales; per-$T$ reporting is the correct fix, flagged in Limitations.)

Pythia size (5 seeds, $\pm$SE): non-monotone, peaking at 410m and dropping at 1b;

\paragraph{Crossover-relative robustness (is the plateau a sampling artifact?).}
A fixed $(r,T)$ probe could sample different points of each model's phase diagram, so we
locate each model's heal$\to$spread crossover by sweeping $T$ at $r{=}2$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:crossover}). \emph{Masked}: base's profile is clearly shifted up (it
amplifies at lower $T$) and tiny is lowest across most of the range, consistent with the
tiny $\ll$ \{mini,\,base\} gap; but the ordering is \emph{not} clean at every $T$ (at
$T{=}0.3$, mini $0.46 <$ tiny $0.51$) and the profiles are single-seed with no error bars.
We therefore read this as a plateau \emph{diagnostic}---the mid-vs-large saturation is a
large-radius averaging effect---rather than an independent confirmation of a monotone
capacity axis. \emph{Autoregressive}: the ordering does \emph{not} hold---Pythia-410m is an outlier (profile
shifted up, lowest crossover) while 70m $\approx$ 160m $\approx$ 1b---so the AR
non-monotonicity is genuine, not a sampling artifact. (The extracted crossover temperature is
itself noisy in the high-variance low-$T$ healing regime---bert-tiny's profile is non-monotone
there---so we compare profiles at fixed $T$ rather than lean on it.)

\subsection{The attractor census}\label{sec:census}
The census is the instrument's read-out of internalized distributional structure; its
ground-truth validation (rung 4 of \S\ref{sec:validation}) is here in full. On three
synthetic first-order Markov sources sharing a vocabulary but with different sparse
transition matrices $P_a/P_b/P_c$, a model trained on each and censused yields an empirical
bigram transition $Q_X$ matching its own $P_X$ (row total-variation $0.22$ vs.\ a
random-lattice baseline $0.91$) and \emph{not} the others (cross-TV $0.95$): the census
recovers each model's own priors quantitatively and discriminates sources
(Fig.~\ref{fig:calib})---the honest trained-model analog of a sampler oracle
\citep{sampler_oracle}. This quantitative recovery is established \emph{only} on the
synthetic toy trained on the exact sources. On real models the census is scored against an
out-of-training WikiText proxy and the numbers are near-floor (top-$50$ overlap
$0.02$--$0.04$, Spearman $0.14$--$0.21$); the largest model's deepest attractors resemble a
corpus \emph{format skeleton}, but the smallest model's are degenerate token fragments. So
the ``the census reads out what the model internalized'' reading is licensed by the
synthetic calibration, not by the real-model overlaps. Vocabulary compression corrupts even
that---a word-level \texttt{<unk>} becomes attractor material and inflates recovery through
artifact-to-artifact matching; byte-level BPE removes it.

\section{The instrument's boundary: token- vs.\ activation-space criticality}\label{sec:crosslevel}
A black-box instrument earns its keep if its readings track something one would otherwise
need internals to see. We tested this hypothesis thoroughly---a ground-truth calibration,
two model families, two within-model axes---and report a clean \textbf{negative}: the
token-space instrument does \emph{not} robustly proxy activation-space criticality.

\textbf{The instrument separates ordered from chaotic rules.} Driving the same
damage-spreading estimator (async, CRN, single-site flip) with 19 classical Elementary CA
rules of \emph{known} class over 12 seeds, the rule-level bootstrap separates ordered from
chaotic decisively ($p<10^{-4}$), while edge-vs-chaotic does not separate ($p{=}0.07$); we
therefore claim only the coarse split. The discriminating quantity is the \emph{ignition
probability} (ordered $0.05$; edge $0.67$; chaotic $0.68$), because single-site damage is
bimodal---it either ignites or dies---so a $\lambda$ averaged over both populations measures
the mixture rather than a growth rate. This is the criticality-side analog of the census
calibration, at the resolution the data support.

\textbf{The white-box reference is a clean, cross-family scaling law.} On the autoregressive
ladder we compute a finite-depth top-Lyapunov exponent
$\lambda_{\mathrm{top}}=\frac{1}{L}\log\rho(J_{\mathrm{emb}\to h_L})$ (the SPARC
error-propagation operator $\rho(F_T)$ \citep{sparc}; finite-difference-JVP power iteration,
fp32). It falls monotonically with size (Pythia 14M--1B, Spearman $\rho{=}-0.94$,
$p{=}0.005$) and this replicates across families (GPT-2 124M--1.5B, $0.152\!\to\!0.059$):
larger models sit nearer critical from the supercritical side.

\textbf{But the black-box instrument does not proxy it} (Fig.~\ref{fig:crosslevel}).
(i)~\emph{Cross-model}, fixed $(r,T)$: the type-matched pairing ($\lambda_{\mathrm{top}}$ vs
$\lambda_{\mathrm{ca}}$) is suggestive on Pythia ($r{=}0.71$, $n{=}6$) but does \emph{not}
replicate on GPT-2 ($r{=}-0.43$); pooling the families gives $r{=}0.70$, $p{=}0.025$, but
that is a Simpson's-paradox artifact of two disagreeing families, not a real effect (the
persistence quantity \dn{} does not track either, $r{=}-0.29$). (ii)~\emph{Within-model
across temperature}: $\lambda_{\mathrm{ca}}(T)$ vs $\rho(F_T)$ gives a uniform
$r\!\approx\!-0.9$ for all six models and both families---a mechanical confound (raising $T$
makes the CA explore more while flattening the softmax), betrayed by its very uniformity,
not cross-level signal. (iii)~\emph{Within-model across radius} (the de-confounded axis,
fixed $T$): the correlation is null (per-model $r$ centred on $0$), because
$\lambda_{\mathrm{ca}}(r)$ is itself \emph{model-invariant}---$\approx[-1.1,0.15,0.5,0.9]$
for $r{=}1,2,4,8$ across every model and family, pure light-cone kinematics carrying no model
signal, while $\rho(F_r)$ varies by model.

\emph{Conclusion:} token-space and activation-space criticality are \textbf{distinct,
non-proxying levels}. The instrument measures a real, calibrated token-space criticality,
but it is not a weights-free surrogate for the activation-space Jacobian---the natural proxy
hypothesis is false, cleanly. The instrument's value is therefore as a \emph{token-space}
measurement in its own right, not as a stand-in for internals.

\textbf{The negative is structural, not a measurement failure.} We mined for a positive along
the principled axes and hit the same barrier each time: $\lambda_{\mathrm{top}}$ is an
\emph{architectural} quantity. Across ten Pythia-410M training checkpoints it is nearly flat
($\sim\!0.13$--$0.19$) while the black-box criticality undergoes a clean order$\to$chaos
transition around step $10^{3}$---the levels do not co-evolve. On a six-model BERT depth
ladder ($L{=}2$--$24$) the white--black correlation is strong ($r{=}-0.92$) but purely
\emph{depth-mediated}: both track depth ($r{=}-0.92$ and $+0.76$), and at fixed depth (two
$L{=}4$ models) $\lambda_{\mathrm{top}}$ is identical while \dn{} differs---so the correlation
conveys only the (public) layer count. Because $\lambda_{\mathrm{top}}\!\approx\!1/L$ is set
by architecture rather than learning, no \emph{useful} weights-free proxy exists with these
measures; the decoupling is a structural fact about the two levels, not an artifact of too
little data.

\section{Related work}
A novelty check (102-agent adversarial harness) places our contribution precisely: the
\emph{instrument} is largely novel at the method level, but its \emph{substrate} is not,
and novelty is uneven across claims. \textbf{Shared substrate (the exposed flank).}
\citet{glauber_mlm} already recasts a masked LM as iterated masked-token resampling---a
Glauber Markov chain on token sequences---the exact substrate we iterate. It measures
mixing time ($O(n\log n)$ high-$T$) and low-$T$ metastability, not a conditioning-radius
axis, light-cone velocity, a damping length, an attractor census, or a finite-size Lyapunov
exponent. The distinction is sharp in the coupling: it tracks Hamming-distance
\emph{contraction} under \emph{maximal} coupling to \emph{bound mixing}, whereas we track
Hamming-distance \emph{spatial propagation} under \emph{common-random-number} coupling to
measure the light cone and a diversity-normalized damping length across a swept radius---
maximal coupling minimizes disagreement (a mixing device), CRN preserves the common noise so
the perturbation's causal spread is visible. Our novelty rests on this measurement layer,
not on the LM-as-iterated-token-CA framing, which we do not claim. \textbf{Edge-of-chaos (claim of \S\ref{sec:repair} is
partially anticipated).} Measuring proximity to the edge of chaos and tying it to
capability is canonical \citep{langton1990computation,bertschinger2004real} and has been
shown for trained transformers via Lyapunov exponents of self-attention Jacobians
\citep{lyap_selfattn}, and named for LLMs generally \citep{edgeofchaos2024,qle}. We claim
neither the concept nor the criticality$\leftrightarrow$capability link; our contribution is
the \emph{token-space}, black-box damping-length measurement and its
certification/calibration protocol, which \citet{lyap_selfattn} (white-box, continuous
hidden space) does not provide. We do \emph{not} claim an established
model-size$\to$sensitivity axis (see \S\ref{sec:repair}). \textbf{The CA light cone is classic (\S3.2 is import, not discovery).} A ballistic
damage front whose velocity is set by the neighborhood range, and the identity
Lyapunov$=$front-velocity, are textbook CA / statistical-physics
\citep{bagnoli1992damage,lieb1972finite,butterfly_ca}; we import them and show the LM
token-lattice exhibits them (behaves as a bona-fide CA) and that the velocity is
model-invariant---not that the law is new. \textbf{Perturbation propagation.}
\citet{sparc} formalizes an error-propagation operator on AR residual streams with a
$\rho(F_T)\!\ge\!1$ criticality threshold (the top-Lyapunov boundary) in activation space,
adjacent but not a token-space CA; \citet{quiver} operates on compound-AI pipeline graphs,
sharing vocabulary but no method. \textbf{Terminology.} We avoid ``repair''---taken by
self-repair / the Hydra effect \citep{hydra,selfrepair} for internal component
compensation---and ``self-correction'' \citep{sparc}, both categorically different from our
spatial damping length. \textbf{Critical sampling temperature (the quantity, not the method, is prior work).}
A per-model critical temperature separating ordered from disordered generation is already
defined and measured across at least five independent works:
\citet{critical_temp,critical_temp2,critical_temp3} locate a $T_c$ by sweeping the softmax
temperature (susceptibility peak, order-parameter collapse, or a measured critical region
$T\!\approx\!1.2$--$1.4$), and \citet{ar_tempcrit,phase_detect} identify the same
temperature-driven order$\to$disorder transition via power-law statistics and phase mapping.
The damage-spreading-to-Lyapunov estimator we use is likewise classical CA physics
\citep{bagnoli1992damage}. We therefore \emph{do not} claim an
edge-of-chaos temperature or a token-space Lyapunov as new quantities; our contribution is a
CRN-certified, ground-truth-calibrated damage-spreading \emph{route} to them (a different
estimator: Lyapunov zero-crossing rather than susceptibility). \textbf{Activation-space
propagation.} \citet{tft} builds an empirical finite-$\epsilon$ position$\times$depth
residual-stream propagation cone and \citet{residual_dynamics} its analytic Jacobian
counterpart; these anticipate the activation-space cone we sketch as future work
(\S\ref{sec:conclusion}), so we treat that direction as a rigor/calibration extension, not a
new object. \textbf{Other.} \citet{sampler_oracle} is the calibration/oracle kin (our
synthetic-Markov recovery is its trained-model analog); the census descends from
\citet{hanson1997computational}; early low-order crystallization is consistent with
distributional simplicity bias \citep{simplicitybias}, read black-box.
\emph{(Pre-submission: full-paper reads of \citet{glauber_mlm,critical_temp,tft} and
\citet{quiver}; verify all 2026-preprint titles/IDs; see
\texttt{results/deep\_research\_novelty*.md}.)}

\section{Limitations}
Masked-LM local conditionals are globally inconsistent, so the CA is a well-defined
stochastic dynamical system but not a sampler of any joint; all claims are properties of
the dynamics. The real-model census is proxy-validated (WikiText, out-of-training), a lower
bound; ground-truth calibration is on synthetic sources only. Rings are small ($N\le384$),
the ladder short (three masked, two autoregressive), and the AR capacity trend rests on two
models with only the sub-saturation radius discriminating (the 410m largest window OOM'd on
16\,GB). The special-token windowing scheme is a first-class apparatus factor to hold fixed;
temperature scales are not comparable across masked and autoregressive constructions.

\textbf{Statistical scope (what the numbers do and do not license).} The paper's central
claims---the validation-ladder reproductions (\S\ref{sec:validation})---are exact or
near-exact matches to known values and do not rest on null-hypothesis testing, so multiple
comparisons are not a concern for them. Where we \emph{do} report trend tests (the cross-level
correlations, \S\ref{sec:crosslevel}), we apply no formal multiplicity correction and treat
them as exploratory: the reported conclusion is a \emph{negative} reached by sign-consistency
across families and axes, not by any single surviving $p$-value, so it is robust to the
correction we omit. The one demoted capacity gap is measured at $N{=}48$ with two seeds and is
not built upon; establishing it as an \emph{axis} would need $\ge15$ independent seeds tested
at the seed level (hierarchical / bootstrap, not a signed-rank over correlated cells), a
longer second-family ladder, multiple $N\gg100$, per-$T$ (not pooled) reporting, and the
diversity-floor normalization ablated against the CRN-null and maximal-coupling floors---all
enumerated in the repository tracker as optional pre-camera-ready work, not load-bearing for
the validated-instrument contribution.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
The contribution of this paper is a \emph{validated} black-box instrument. Its organizing
principle---validation by reproduction---is what separates it from the qualitative
iterated-LLM literature: before measuring a language model, the CA/damage-spreading apparatus
recovers the criticality classes of elementary CA rules and the known transition matrices of
synthetic Markov sources---the rungs that share its regime of discrete state and finite
perturbation---while the smooth-limit logistic and coupled-map rungs are retained, and
labelled, as arithmetic unit tests whose agreement is an $O(\epsilon)$ limit property
(\S\ref{sec:validation}). An instrument that recovers what is independently known, and that
is explicit about which of its checks are load-bearing, can be trusted on what is not. Pointed at
trained LMs, it then yields weights-free measurements a next-token evaluation does not
(\S\ref{sec:lm}): a token-space Lyapunov exponent and a diversity-controlled damping length of
generation, an effective interaction radius ($r{\approx}4$), a model-invariant damage light
cone, and an attractor census of internalized structure---each certified by the exact-zero-null
CRN protocol and, where ground truth exists, calibrated against it. We also draw the
instrument's \emph{boundary} (\S\ref{sec:crosslevel}): a controlled cross-level study returns a
clean negative---the black-box token-space criticality does not proxy the white-box
activation-space Lyapunov, because the latter is architectural---so token- and activation-space
criticality are distinct, non-proxying levels. The phenomena the instrument measures are old
and deep; what is new is a trustworthy way to measure them from the outside, and an honest map
of where that measurement reaches its limit.

\paragraph{Opportunities and future work.} The instrument reads a model's dynamics from
\emph{token space alone}---no weights, gradients, or activations---which makes it usable in
the black-box / API-only regime. \emph{Applications.} (i)~The damping length, light cone, and
critical sampling temperature \citep{critical_temp} are measurable on closed models where
activation-space probes are unavailable; our cross-level study (\S\ref{sec:crosslevel}) maps
precisely how far this black-box reading can and cannot substitute for internals.
(ii)~The attractor census, calibrated to recover a \emph{known} transition matrix
(\S\ref{sec:census}), is a quantitative probe of the distributional structure a model has
internalized \citep{hanson1997computational}. (iii)~The effective interaction radius
($r\!\approx\!4$) can be correlated with attention-head span or induction heads to link a
black-box observable to mechanism. (iv)~CRN damage cones are a causal-influence map over
token positions, and the damping length bounds how far a single-token error propagates before
absorption---relevant to error attribution and hallucination. (v)~The training-time
trajectory (F25) is a phase-change detector across checkpoints for developmental
interpretability. \emph{Extensions of the same validated method.} (vi)~A second,
\emph{compositional-complexity} axis---an effective-rule-complexity or assembly-theoretic
read-out of what the dynamics \emph{produce}, complementary to the criticality of how they
\emph{behave}---would be validated the same way, against strings of known complexity.
(vii)~Applying the damage-spreading machinery to the residual stream gives an activation-space
information-propagation cone; that object is closely anticipated \citep{tft,residual_dynamics},
so the contribution there would be the CRN certification and ground-truth calibration, not the
cone itself. (viii)~The deterministic (greedy, $T\!\to\!0$) limit is a distinct dynamical
object worth measuring, to test whether the criticality readings are a sampling phenomenon.
\emph{Rigor.} The census recovery, quantitative on synthetic sources, should be re-established
against a real model's \emph{true} training corpus rather than a proxy; and the demoted
capacity gap, if revisited, needs the seed-level rebuild set out in Limitations. Throughout,
the reusable asset is the statistics-level, apparatus-nulled certification protocol, which
applies to \emph{any} iterated-LLM system---self-refinement, agentic loops, iterated
rewriting---where trajectory-level divergence otherwise saturates for signal and apparatus
alike. A Lieb--Robinson-style propagation bound \citep{lieb1972finite} for the non-unitary
stochastic LM dynamics would put the light cone on rigorous footing.

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}
All code, checkpoints, per-run results, and figures are in the \texttt{token-lattice-ca}
repository; each headline maps to a finding \texttt{F$k$} in \texttt{findings.md}. Hardware:
Apple M1 Pro, 16\,GB; toy model on CPU JAX, real models fp16 on the MPS backend.

\textbf{Models.} \emph{Toy}: a 0.42\,M-parameter bidirectional transformer ($d{=}96$,
$4$ heads, $2$ layers) trained from scratch on tinyshakespeare (word-level 2000 vocab, and
a 4096 byte-level BPE) as a joint windowed-conditional model over $r\in\{1,2,4,8,16\}$,
$6000$ Adam steps. \emph{Masked}: \texttt{prajjwal1/bert-tiny} (2L/128), \texttt{-mini}
(4L/256), \texttt{bert-base-uncased} (12L/768), shared bert-base-uncased WordPiece vocab
(30522); loaded as \texttt{BertForMaskedLM}. \emph{Autoregressive}: \texttt{EleutherAI/
pythia-70m/160m/410m}, causal window $p(x_i\mid x_{i-r..i-1})$.

\textbf{Rule / automaton.} Async random-order single-site Glauber on a ring of $N$ cells;
inverse-CDF sampling from the tempered, special-token-forbidden center (masked) or
next-token (causal) distribution, computed on-device. CRN twins share init, visitation
order, and the uniform stream; the two twins are run as one $2B$ batch with tiled uniforms
(bit-identical to two runs). Null test: exactly zero divergence (\texttt{tests/}).

\textbf{Grids and seeds.} Phase sweep: $N{=}48$, $B{=}16$, $40$ sweeps, $T\in\{0.5,0.8,1.0,
1.3,1.6,2.0,2.5\}$, $r\in\{1,2,4,8,16\}$; multi-seed sweep uses $5$ seeds. Radius
certification (F20/F22): $N{=}48$, $B{=}16$, $5$ seeds, $T{=}0.7$, schemes cls\_sep and
no-special. Velocity FSS (F21): $N\in\{48,96,192,384\}$, $r\in\{4,8,16\}$, $B{=}24$. Damping
length (F23): $N{=}48$, $B{=}32$, settle $12$, $25$--$30$ sweeps, $2$ seeds, $T\in\{0.5,0.7,
0.9\}$, $r\in\{1,2,4,8,16\}$, tail $8$; the diversity floor $D_0$ is the same-init,
independent-noise, no-flip drift (a full radius-$r$ run, hence velocity-matched). Finite-size
Lyapunov: least-squares slope of $\log(\text{damaged sites})$ over the ballistic window.
Calibration (\S3.5): three first-order Markov sources, $K{=}60$ symbols, $6$ successors,
$300$\,k tokens each, a tiny model per source ($4000$ steps); recovery is row total-variation
between the censused and true transition matrix. Census on real models is proxy-validated
against a WikiText-103 slice re-tokenized per model ($\sim$240--250\,k tokens).

\textbf{Statistics.} F12 (crossover), F21 (velocity), and the F23 radius trend are
finite-size scanned. The F23 capacity gap is measured with \emph{two} seeds; the
$15/15$-cell signed-rank we originally reported ($p<10^{-4}$) is pseudoreplicated
(correlated $(r,T)$ cells driven by the same two seeds) and is \emph{not} treated as a
significance claim---the defensible statement is the tiny $\ll$ \{mini,\,base\} gap
(base-vs-mini null, $8/15$). A seed-level rebuild ($\ge15$ independent seeds, hierarchical /
bootstrap test) is the tracked pre-camera-ready fix.

\textbf{Known limits of this run.} The AR capacity trend uses two--three Pythia sizes with
one discriminating radius; the $410$m largest-window cell OOM'd on 16\,GB and was dropped;
the real-model census is a proxy; reference metadata in \texttt{refs.bib} is web-verified but
2026 preprints may change.
