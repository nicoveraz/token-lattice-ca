# Critical analysis: textca as an interpretability tool

**Date:** 1 August 2026.
**Scope:** an outside critical read of the project with one question in front: how far is this from being a *tool for interpretability*, and what would developing it into one require. Based on `README.md`, `findings.md` (F1–F76), `paper/paper.tex`, `paper/REVIEW.md`, `paper/plan_paper2.md`, `assembly_theory.md`, `ca_constructions.md`, `tracker_dump.md`, the `src/` and `tests/` trees, and a sample of `experiments/`. Weaknesses the project has already recorded about itself are credited as such; the aim here is synthesis, prioritization, and the interpretability lens specifically, not rediscovery.

---

## 1. Verdict in brief

The project has built two things of real value: a *validated black-box measurement apparatus* for iterated token dynamics, and — more unusual — a *working falsification discipline* that caught six confident wrong verdicts before any reached a paper (F56, F57, F59-v1, F61, F65, F66) and that keeps every retraction visible in place. The Domany–Kinzel bit-exact rung, the exact-zero CRN null asserted on every backend, the pre-registered blind gates that return `NOT DECIDABLE`, and the self-auditing test suite are close to state of the art for a solo research repo.

What the project has *not* built, on its own evidence, is an interpretability tool. The submission venue (NeurIPS 2026 *Interpretability for Discovery*) states the aspiration, but the strongest results in `findings.md` cut the three links that would make the instrument interpretive rather than phenomenological. F35 severs the link to deployed generation: a real autoregressive continuation absorbs *no* injected token error (P_persist = 1.000 on three models), so damping, healing, and D_norm characterize the iterated-resampling construction, not the model's generative process. F26/F28/F29 sever the link to internals: black-box token-space criticality does not proxy white-box activation-space criticality, structurally, and the log correctly closes that line ("Do not re-run"). F62–F70 sever much of the link to "the model" at all: the AR critical point the universality programme spent its compute on turned out to be the melting of an out-of-distribution prompt degeneracy — confined to r ∈ {1,2}, carried by a single vocabulary entry, two-thirds removed by a single BOS token, absent in the native masked construction.

The honest current state is therefore: a high-integrity instrument that measures a *(model, construction)* pair, whose surviving model-facing claim is one comparative training-time result (the developmental transition, F39/F46), plus a large body of negative knowledge about probes. That is a legitimate scientific position — arguably a distinguished one — but it is a *pre-tool* position. A tool must give a user a model-level fact they can act on, and the repo does not yet demonstrate one. The rest of this document is about the gap and how to close it.

---

## 2. What is genuinely strong (and why it matters for interpretability specifically)

It would be a misreading of this analysis to conclude the project is weak. The epistemic machinery is the project's moat, and it addresses a failure mode interpretability research is known to suffer from: results that are artifacts of the probe rather than properties of the model. The field has accumulated illusion results around probing classifiers, activation patching, and evaluation of explanations; this repo demonstrates, end to end, what defense looks like: calibrate the estimator at the geometry it will be used at (F56), state and test the independent unit of measurement (F57), reject fits that land on the scan edge (F59), run a control that should show nothing (F65), and vary the construction, not only the subject (F66) — the last one being the sharpest, since nineteen models could not distinguish "a property of LMs" from "a property of the probe" and one change of apparatus settled it immediately.

Concretely worth preserving: the `Rule` protocol with one shared simulation loop so a stub rule exercises the production code path by construction; the DK rung asserted cell-for-cell through that same loop, with an off-line control that must fail so the test cannot pass vacuously; provenance stamps that turn stale-analysis incidents into red tests; the paper-number manifest tying every literal in `paper.tex` to a results file; and a findings ledger in which eleven retracted claims remain readable. F62–F70 themselves, read as a unit, are a publishable cautionary result *for* the interpretability community: iterated self-consumption probes can manufacture a precise, reproducible, exponent-bearing phase transition that belongs entirely to the probe.

The critique that follows takes all of this as given.

## 3. The central weakness: a detector with no explanandum

Interpretability, on any working definition, explains behavior in terms of what is inside the model, or at minimum produces model-level facts that support explanation, prediction, or intervention. Measured against that bar, each surviving observable falls short in a specific, documented way:

| Observable | What survives | Scope limit (project's own finding) |
|---|---|---|
| λ_ca (token-space Lyapunov) | Developmental sub→super-critical transition; intensive across N (N^−0.04) | Comparative-across-checkpoints only; valid at T ∈ {0.5, 0.7}; construction-scoped (F35); "we do not claim any model *is* critical" |
| D_norm (damping length) | Corroborates the transition at stated N | Scales as 1/N (F45); floor-coupling mismatch disclosed, not dissolved (W2/F48); construction-scoped |
| Light-cone velocity | Grows with r | Model-invariant — carries no model signal (F16/F21/F28); apparatus kinematics |
| Attractor census | Recovers known Markov matrices | Real-model rung near-floor against a corpus the models never saw (W5, issue #6) |
| Attractor share / T\* | Graded 6–98% across 26 models; corpus-sensitive | A property of an OOD two-token prompt regime (F65/F66); external anchor unresolved (F68: family-level p = 0.189, n = 9) |
| Cross-level proxy | — | Clean structural negative (F26/F28/F29) |
| Assembly Δ | — | Closed by its own pre-registered null; the per-object assembly term is "irrelevant" (F75/F76) |

Two consequences follow.

**First, the paper's stated affordance is asserted, not demonstrated.** The responsible-use section claims the instrument "characterises models behind an API, where weights are unavailable and activation-space methods cannot reach." But nowhere in the repo does an API-style measurement deliver a fact about a specific model that a practitioner would act on. The one clear case of the probe reading genuine model-level signal from behavior alone is buried inside the artifact programme: gpt-neo-125M and gpt2 share an identical tokenizer and differ only in corpus, and the probe separates them 78.1% vs 20.4% (F64). That is a real black-box inference about *training data* from behavior — and it is currently used only as a control in a negative-result argument, not developed as a capability.

**Second, the flagship result is a *when*, not a *what*.** The developmental transition says token dynamics reorganize between steps 256 and 512 for Pythia-410m/1b (128–256 for 160m), robustly (0 of 48 plateau runs negative, four pre-registered family members surviving BH-FDR, replicated at three lattice sizes and four model sizes). What it does not say is what changed inside the model — which is precisely the question an interpretability venue will ask. The repo already knows the leading candidate and has open issues for it (#69/#70: induction-head formation timing within the Pythia family) — unexecuted. As long as the transition is unconnected to any independently measurable internal event, λ_ca is a detector without an explanandum: it flags that *something* reorganized, and interpretability starts where that sentence ends.

There is also a latent vulnerability worth naming before a reviewer does: the developmental family is measured on the same r = 2 AR construction that F65 showed is uniquely artifact-prone ("the model-distinguishing frozen phase exists only at the two-token window the entire project uses"). The submitted paper defends the temperature axis — T = 0.7 is far from the T ≈ 0.436 artifact, and the construction is held fixed across checkpoints, so a change across checkpoints cannot come from the apparatus. Both arguments are sound. But no result in the ledger shows the *developmental* transition surviving at r = 3 or 4, where one extra token of context removes the degeneracy (F69). A skeptic's cheapest attack is "your flagship lives at the radius your own interventions condemned." One replication of the developmental family at r = 3–4 (or on the clean MLM construction across a BERT-style training run) closes it.

## 4. Weaknesses in the surviving science

**Single family, confounded axis.** The developmental result rests on one model family. The learning-rate/size confound is conceded and correctly reported as an ordering rather than a size effect, and the iso-LR width scan (#83) plus a second checkpointed family (#61) are already tracked as prerequisites — but tracked is not done, and until one lands, "training moves the token lattice from sub- to super-critical" is a Pythia fact, not an LM fact. Note the awkward position the artifact programme leaves this in: the second AR family examined (GPT-2) turned out to lack public intermediate checkpoints for a developmental replication and to lack the frozen phase entirely, and the MLM construction — the clean one — has no absorbing transition to track (F67). The generality of the flagship is genuinely untested.

**A two-point temperature window.** The effect is detectable at T = 0.5 and 0.7 and saturates at 0.3 (floor) and 0.9/1.1 (ceiling) (F49/F52). The thermometer analogy in the log is fair — an instrument with a range is still an instrument — but a range of two grid points, with the mechanism of the window itself (ignition floor/ceiling) being a property of the probe, is thin ground for a general claim. The window's location is also unexplained in model terms.

**Disclosed debts that remain open.** W2 stands: D_norm divides a matched-coupling numerator by an unmatched-coupling floor; F48 bounded the arbitrariness at ~4% and showed the CRN floor is an isolated point, which converts the objection from "invalid" to "irreducible but small" — yet D_norm remains a diagnostic ratio whose absolute value means nothing off one lattice size. W5 stands: real-model census recovery is scored against a corpus the models never saw (issue #6, open). F60 stands: a reproducible ~7% downward bias in the collapse estimator for ladders reaching N ≥ 96, six hypotheses refuted, cause unknown — every future z-type claim inherits this disclosure. The 1b step-256 anomaly (λ = −0.546) is unexplained. None of these is hidden; all of them are load these instruments carry into any future use.

**Program-level selection risk.** Of the three post-submission programmes, the universality programme died as a probe artifact (the discipline worked, but only after ~17 h and ~44 h production runs plus the screening cascade), the assembly programme closed on its own pre-registered null with the honest verdict that Δ is "close to a tautology" and "not a result about assembly theory" (F75/F76), and T\* remains underpowered. The falsification layer is excellent; the *hypothesis-generation* layer keeps proposing physics-first questions whose relevance to models is discovered only afterwards, usually negatively. An interpretability tool needs the opposite ordering: start from a question about models that someone outside the project already cares about, then ask whether the instrument can answer it. The tracker contains exactly such questions (#58: does the variance collapse anticipate known capability emergences; #84: λ_ca vs held-out loss collapse; #69/#70: induction heads) — they are consistently the ones deferred.

**Residual forking-paths exposure.** Seventy-six findings on substantially shared data, models, and one hardware budget. Pre-registration, BH-FDR, and the unit-of-analysis discipline mitigate per-claim risk, and the log itself names the danger (F68: "'collect more data until p drops' would have declared victory"). But the project-level garden of forking paths — many estimators, many constructions, many re-reads of the same Pythia checkpoints — cannot be corrected away, only disclosed. A second family is the real answer here too.

## 5. Weaknesses as a tool (engineering)

The README describes a "validated black-box measurement instrument," and the validation half of that phrase is earned. The instrument half is not yet an artifact anyone else could pick up. This section is blunt because the fix is mostly mechanical.

**Nobody can install it.** There is no `pyproject.toml`, no pinned environment, no CI; imports resolve through a `sys.path` shim repeated at the top of ~20 files, and the modules carry maximally collision-prone names (`model`, `ca`, `lattice`). Configuration is editing constants at the top of scripts (`dp_fss_z.py`: `BASE, REVISION`, `SIZES`, `SEEDS_FOR = {96: SEEDS[:2]}`), and one backend is configured by monkeypatching a mutable module global (`ca.DATA_DIR` — flagged as a hazard in issue #25, open). Device handling assumes Apple Silicon (`pick_device(prefer="mps")`, `caffeinate` in usage strings, fp16-on-MPS defaults); golden files were generated at fp16 on one MPS machine, so the bit-identity guarantee — correctly treasured — silently does not transfer to CUDA or CPU adopters.

**The library/script boundary is inverted.** `src/` is clean and small (the `lattice.run` unification with a written RNG-consumption contract is exactly right), but the de facto library extends deep into `experiments/`: `provenance`, `dp_calibration`, `mlm_lib`, and — worst placed — `lyapunov.py`, where the project's single most load-bearing analysis predicate (`run_ignited`, "The single definition; do not re-implement it") lives *after* the `if __name__ == "__main__"` block of a CLI script whose import side-effects set environment variables and pull torch. The ~110-script `experiments/` directory manages consistency across its name families (`dev_transition_*` ×8, `crosslevel_*` ×5, `dp_*` ×7) not through shared functions but through bespoke pairwise consistency tests — the suite is partly a compensator for missing shared code, and the ledger records the cost (two scripts applying one filter rule differently moved the headline quantity 14%, F45). Even the guard has been outflanked in-repo: `build_paper_manifest.py` imports `is_unignited as _unig` and hand-writes the mean_damage/D_norm dispatch adapter around it three times in one file — semantically safe because the predicate itself is imported, but exactly the adapter-shape proliferation the F42 guard test exists to prevent, slipping past it the way its own docstring predicted ("renaming a local helper is how the fourteenth copy would slip past").

**The test suite freezes a paper, not a tool.** The most distinctive tests pin *conclusions*: the 160m crossing bracket must equal `["step128","step256"]`; Phase 3 must contain exactly one unignited run. For a submission tag this is a ratchet of real value; for a tool it means any new data breaks the suite by design. Meanwhile the components a tool user would rely on have no correctness tests at all: no synthetic-known-λ test for `lyap_from_cone`, nothing for `fit_z`/collapse cost (the component that produced the withdrawn z), nothing for the hand-rolled `bh_fdr`/`_spearman`/`cohens_d` that duplicate scipy — in one case in a file that already imports scipy. Provenance stamps hash only the top-level script — not its imports (a change to `run_ignited` invalidates every consumer without tripping any stamp) and not the environment (no package versions recorded in any results file).

**The cost wall blocks the science's own next steps.** The inner loop makes one model forward pass per site visit; there is no batching across windows, no caching, no exercised CUDA path. The repo's own tables put a single N=192 cell at ~143 h on the available hardware, and the 16 GB budget already forced the N·B = 768 confound that cost one finding (F44/F47). Every improvement recommended in this document — second family, larger N, r = 3–4 replication, more seeds — runs through this wall. Engineering throughput is not a nicety here; it is the binding constraint on the remaining scientific questions.

**Documentation teaches the science, not the tool.** The README is an exemplary findings ledger and reproduction guide for *this* project's results. There is no "point this at your model" path: no API documentation, no quickstart, no statement of which components are stable versus paper-frozen.

## 6. What "developing it into an interpretability tool" would take

The recommendations are ordered by leverage. The first three are science; the last three are engineering and positioning. Items already present in the tracker are marked, because part of the critique is that the highest-value items are already known and consistently deferred.

**6.1 Decide what the product is.** Three honest candidates coexist in the repo and pull in different directions: (a) a *training-dynamics monitor* — a cheap black-box phase detector for model developers; (b) a *probe-artifact audit kit* — the falsification methodology plus the F62–F70 taxonomy, packaged for people who probe models with iterated inference; (c) a *mechanistic bridge* — the research bet that dynamics observables can be connected to internals. (a) and (b) are within reach of the existing evidence; (c) failed once structurally (F26–F29) and would need a genuinely new idea, not a re-run. Pick explicitly; the paper currently gestures at all three, and the "characterises models behind an API" sentence writes a check that only (a) or a fingerprinting variant of it can cash.

**6.2 Give the thermometer a disease** (tracker #58, #84). Reviewer 1's phrase — "a well-built thermometer never shown to track a disease" — remains the sharpest one-line critique after all the hardening, because everything retained is instrument-facing. The single highest-value experiment available today: take the λ_ca crossing and the variance collapse (Levene p ≤ 1.7×10⁻⁴, currently "reported as an observation") and test them as *predictors* against independently measured events across the same public checkpoints — benchmark discontinuities, in-context-learning onset, induction-head formation, held-out loss structure (#84). A positive result converts the instrument from phenomenology into an early-warning diagnostic that model developers would actually run during training, and it would be the first result in the repo that an outside practitioner could *use*. A negative result scopes the tool honestly at the cost of a few GPU-days. Either way the thermometer finally gets pointed at a patient.

**6.3 Build the internal bridge where it is cheap** (tracker #69/#70). The crossing brackets (128–256 for 160m; 256–512 for 410m/1b) plausibly overlap the early-training window where induction heads form in small transformers, and prefix-matching scores across public Pythia checkpoints are an afternoon of work with weights the project already downloads. If the λ_ca crossing co-times with the induction bump per size — including the *ordering* across sizes, which λ_ca measures and which is the project's distinctive datum — the developmental transition acquires a mechanistic interpretation and the instrument becomes, at minimum, a black-box correlate of a white-box event. That is the missing external-validity result restated in achievable form, and unlike the failed λ_top proxy it targets a discrete, well-documented internal event rather than a continuous spectral quantity. The incidental white-box assets (the λ_top ≈ 1/L architectural law, replicated across two families; the activation-cone prototype, prior-art-checked in #19) belong to this same thread: develop them as measurement or park them explicitly.

**6.4 Establish generality or concede scope** (tracker #61, #83, #16). One checkpointed non-Pythia family (OLMo-class or LLM360-class releases with intermediate checkpoints) decides whether the flagship is an LM fact or a Pythia fact, and the iso-depth iso-LR width scan breaks the confound the paper currently reports around. Add the two construction robustness checks this document has argued are exposed: the developmental family at r = 3–4 (§3), and — if it can be afforded — a from-scratch masked-LM training run to test the transition on the construction F66 certified as clean. Issue #16 (windowed vs full-context rule) is the deeper version of the same question and would also test whether the instrument's readings become *more* model-like as the construction approaches the model's native regime.

**6.5 Aim the instrument at the population it actually matches** (tracker #59). F35's boundary — real generation never resamples — is a statement about *today's autoregressive decoders*. Diffusion language models resample in place by construction; for that class, the iterated-resampling construction is not a probe artifact but a sketch of the deployed process, and the entire damping/healing/criticality vocabulary plausibly applies literally. The tracker already contains this observation and nothing has been done with it. Measuring one open diffusion LM would give the instrument its first population where F35 dissolves — and where "token-space dynamics" is not a construction but the product. Separately, the corpus-sensitivity result (gpt-neo vs gpt2, F64) points to a second concrete capability: black-box *fingerprinting* — family attribution, training-corpus inference, possibly distillation detection — behind APIs. That is model characterization rather than interpretation, but it is the one demonstrated case of the probe reading training-level signal from behavior, and it has users.

**6.6 Pay the engineering debt in one deliberate pass.** Extract the reusable core into an installable package: `lattice.run` + the `Rule` protocol, the four backends, the estimator/predicate layer (`run_ignited`, λ and D_norm estimators, the DP calibration gates), and `provenance` — with configuration objects instead of module constants, a device abstraction with an exercised CUDA path, pinned dependencies, and CI that runs the StubRule suite (no HF downloads needed, by design — an existing strength). Separate the paper-freeze tests (conclusion pins, golden files, manifest checks) into the tagged paper tree where they belong, and give the tool tests the missing correctness layer: synthetic known-λ cones, known-z collapses, scipy-checked stats. Extend provenance stamps to the import closure and record package versions in results files. Then write the fifteen-minute guide: *measure λ_ca across your checkpoints*. None of this is research; approximately all of it is what "tool" means. The instrument's throughput wall (one forward per site visit) deserves one focused optimization effort — batched window evaluation across sites in sync mode, batched replicas everywhere, mixed precision on CUDA — because §6.2–6.4 are compute-bound on it.

**6.7 Ship the discipline as a product of its own** (tracker #60). The most transferable thing in this repo may not be λ_ca but the pattern language around it: calibrate at the measurement's own geometry; return `NOT DECIDABLE`; blind the gate to the answer; include an anti-vacuity control that must fail; state the independent unit and test it; stamp analyses with their own hash; pin retracted claims so they cannot return. Interpretability pipelines — SAE evaluations, probing studies, activation-patching experiments — fail in exactly the ways this machinery detects, and nothing like a standard exists there. A short methods paper plus a small library of these guards, with F62–F70 as the worked example of a manufactured phenomenon caught by construction-variation, is plausibly the project's most citable contribution and requires no new experiments at all. The second paper plan (claim B: "gating the estimator at the measurement's own geometry... the transferable methods contribution") already contains this insight; the recommendation is to treat it as the headline for the interpretability audience, not the consolation prize.

## 7. Bottom line

The project set out to measure language models with physics and ended up, by its own unflinching accounting, measuring mostly its probe — while building, along the way, one of the more trustworthy measurement-and-falsification stacks in the space. As interpretability, the current deliverables are one robust but unexplained *when* (the developmental transition, one family, two temperatures, one radius), one incidental white-box law, a corpus-sensitive OOD fallback taxonomy with no external anchor yet, and a body of negative results that are individually excellent and collectively define the tool's boundary rather than its use.

The gap to "tool for interpretability" is not primarily statistical rigor — that battle has been won conspicuously — it is *referent* and *user*: something the numbers are about that lives inside or generalizes beyond the construction, and someone outside the project who can act on them. The three cheapest bridges are already in the tracker: predictive validity against capability emergence (#58/#84), induction-head co-timing (#69/#70), and the diffusion-LM population where the construction is the deployment (#59). If one of them lands, the instrument earns the word; if none does, the honest terminal state is a methods-and-cautionary contribution — genuinely valuable to interpretability, but then the framing, the README's first paragraph, and the next paper should say so in those words.
